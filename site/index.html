Rereading feels like studying.
Testing yourself works better.

Answering questions and reviewing a few days later beats rereading your slides (the research agrees). Flashcards help, but they take hours to write and explain nothing on their own. MDlearn gives you both: full notes with the exercises built in.

How it works

  1. Add your files

    PDFs, slides, Word documents, notes. Or just a topic.

  2. The AI writes the notes

    With examples, formulas and exercises. MDlearn checks every question has a right answer.

  3. Review 5–10 minutes a day

    The app brings each idea back as your memory of it starts to fade.

No more copying slides by hand

Add everything at once: the AI turns it into a course, split by topic.

Notes you can actually learn from

Formulas explained term by term, plots, diagrams and animations, not bullet points.

No more lucky guesses

Before the answer, the app asks if you were sure. Guessed? The question comes back sooner.

Hard to read? Change the font.

Pick an easier-to-read font or one for dyslexia, at any size. Then highlight in six colours and add your own notes.

Your notes are yours

  • No account

    Nothing to sign up for. This site doesn't track you either.

  • Plain files

    Your notes are text files on your computer. They open even without MDlearn.

  • You choose the AI

    An online AI sees the files you give it. To keep everything offline, use a local AI with Ollama.

Download

The app is new and has no paid digital signature yet, so Windows or macOS may warn you at first. That's normal, and the code is public on GitHub.

Windows

Installer (.exe)

"Windows protected your PC" → More info → Run anyway.

macOS

Apple chip (.dmg)Intel (.dmg)

Drag it to Applications, open it once, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Linux

.AppImage.deb

Make the AppImage executable, then open it.

Commands

chmod +x MDlearn-*.AppImage (needs libfuse2), or install the .deb: sudo apt install ./MDlearn-*.deb

Terminal

npx mdlearn studio

Opens MDlearn in your browser. Needs Node.js 22.15 or newer.

Verify (optional)

SHA256SUMS.txt

Check that your file matches the list.

How

Windows: certutil -hashfile FILE SHA256
macOS and Linux: shasum -a 256 FILE

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. It's open source, under the MIT license.

Windows says the app isn't recognised. Is it safe?

The app isn't code-signed yet, so Windows doesn't know it. Click More info → Run anyway. The code is public on GitHub, and you can check your download against SHA256SUMS.txt.

If Smart App Control is on, Windows 11 blocks unsigned apps with no "Run anyway" button. Signing the app is on the roadmap.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The desktop app is all you need: no terminal, no Node.js.

Which AI do I need?

One you already use: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini… The regular chat version works too.

Which languages?

The app is in English, Italian, Spanish, French and Catalan. Courses can be in any language.

Does it work on my phone?

You can study from your phone over your home network, but the app runs on your computer. Open this page there to download it.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your disk. See privacy.
